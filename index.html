piLynk

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

piLynk is a local file and text-sharing application that allows users to share content between devices over a local network.

Your privacy matters. piLynk is designed to let users share content directly from their device without requiring a cloud account or cloud storage service, or any thing at all.

2. Information Collection

piLynk does not collect personal information, or any information at all, or require users to create an account.

Files and text selected for sharing are served from the user's device so that other devices can access them through the local network. piLynk does not upload this content to anywhere like piLynk-operated cloud server.

3. Data Sharing

piLynk does not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers.

When you use the app to share files or text, the content may be accessible to other devices that can reach the local sharing server. Only share content with devices and people you trust.

4. Network Access and Security

The app uses (wifi LAN) network access to provide its local sharing functionality. Users are responsible for enabling sharing intentionally and stopping the local server when it is no longer needed.

Local network sharing does not guarantee that transferred content is encrypted. Avoid sharing sensitive information over untrusted networks.

5. Third-Party Services

piLynk does not intentionally transmit user content to third-party analytics or advertising services. If third-party services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

6. Children's Privacy

piLynk is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or ask users to submit personal information.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when app functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the same URL.
